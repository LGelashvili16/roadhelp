# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Drivers, vehicle owners, and vehicle buyers who need a car collected or transported in Tbilisi, elsewhere in Georgia, or to a nearby country by prior arrangement.

## Product Purpose

RoadHelp explains the available flatbed transport service, its coverage, and the handling process, then gives visitors a direct way to call or contact the driver through WhatsApp.

## Positioning

RoadHelp focuses on carrying the whole vehicle on a flatbed, with all four wheels off the road, for city, intercity, and arranged cross-border routes.

## Operating Context

Visitors may be arranging transport for a damaged, non-running, low-clearance, valuable, or newly purchased vehicle. They need to share the pickup location, vehicle model, and destination before the route and transport details can be agreed.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Vehicle evacuation and transport within Tbilisi.
- Vehicle transport throughout Georgia.
- Cross-border transport to nearby countries only by prior arrangement.
- The website is Georgian-first, with complete English and Russian versions.
- The site is static and prioritizes click-to-call and WhatsApp contact.
- Roadside repair, tire changes, fuel delivery, vehicle storage, and unconfirmed availability claims are outside scope.
- Prices, response times, exact destinations, operating hours, and a public address remain unconfirmed and must not be invented.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: RoadHelp.
- Production domain: `roadhelp.ge`.
- Georgian is the primary language; English and Russian are supported equally.
- Voice is direct, calm, practical, and careful rather than exaggerated or urgent.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing service copy and contact placeholders: `src/content/site.ts`.
- Existing truck photography: `src/assets/roadhelp-tbilisi-hero.png` and `src/assets/roadhelp-region.png`.
- Existing public social image: `public/social-card.jpg`.
- No verified testimonials, certifications, service statistics, prices, response-time promises, or operating-hour claims are available.

## Product Principles

- Explain the service and its limits plainly.
- Make calling the driver the clearest next action.
- Treat careful vehicle handling as a process that can be shown, not an unsupported promise.
- Keep every public page complete and equivalent in Georgian, English, and Russian.
- Prefer verified operational facts over generic towing-industry claims.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic structure, keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus states, localized alternative text, reduced-motion support, and layouts that tolerate Georgian and Russian text expansion.
